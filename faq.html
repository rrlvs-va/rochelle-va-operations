Experience & fit

How much professional experience do you have?

I have 18 years of professional experience across executive and administrative support, business development, virtual assistance, customer and CRM operations, outbound sales and lead qualification, research, documentation, and creative support.

What parts of your previous work experience apply to your VA and operations services today?

My previous roles involved organizing information, supporting executives and teams, handling customer communication, maintaining records and CRM notes, researching information, preparing documents and presentations, following up on tasks, qualifying leads, and keeping work moving across people and systems.

Do you specialize in one industry?

No. My experience crosses different types of support rather than one single industry. I can adapt to a client's environment when the work fits my skills and any industry-specific process or training needed for the role is provided.

Do you offer ongoing support as well as one-time projects?

Yes. Work can be arranged as hourly support, a defined project, or an ongoing retainer depending on scope, timeline, budget, and available capacity.

Can we start with a limited paid trial before committing to ongoing support?

Yes. A paid trial can be set around a defined task, time period, or scope with an agreed budget and clear end point. There is no obligation to purchase additional work after the trial is complete.

What information helps you scope a request accurately?

The most useful information is the outcome you need, tasks involved, deadline, tools or accounts involved, what has already been done, what still needs a decision, and whether the work is one-time or ongoing.

Communication, schedule & tools

Do you work with clients in different time zones?

Yes. I am based in the Philippines and can support international clients. Most work is handled independently and asynchronously, so full-day overlap is usually unnecessary.

What are your normal response times?

My general working-day message response standard is within 24 hours. Faster response expectations or scheduled coverage should be agreed separately.

Do I need you online all day, or do you work asynchronously?

My normal approach is async-first. Tasks, updates, files, and next steps are documented so progress does not depend on everyone being online at the same time.

Can live meetings, calls, or scheduled coverage be arranged?

Yes, when needed and arranged in advance. Onboarding calls, check-ins, reviews, meetings, or scheduled coverage can be built around the agreed engagement.

How do you handle availability and client capacity?

I confirm availability before accepting work and avoid treating reserved time as unlimited capacity. The website gives 15–20 hours per week as a general reference depending on each client contract.

Can you work within the tools and systems my business already uses?

Usually, yes. I prefer working within an existing setup where practical. Appropriate access, permissions, instructions, and any client-specific training should be provided when needed.

What happens if something becomes urgent or needs rush handling?

Send the actual deadline and explain what makes it urgent. I will confirm whether I can accommodate it based on current workload and existing commitments before proceeding.

Pricing, trials & changes

How do hourly, retainer, and project-based arrangements differ?

Hourly support works for changing workloads. A retainer reserves agreed ongoing capacity within a monthly scope. Project-based work is tied to a defined deliverable or outcome.

Do retainers include unlimited work?

No. A retainer reserves agreed capacity; it does not mean unlimited tasks or unlimited availability.

Can payments be split or scheduled?

They may be, depending on the project. A split payment or installment arrangement can be discussed before work begins.

Are payments refundable?

Payments are generally non-refundable once work has begun, time or capacity has been reserved, or a project has been completed. Any refund required by applicable law would still be honored.

What happens if a project or ongoing arrangement is cancelled?

Cancellation terms depend on the arrangement. Work already completed, time already used or reserved, and approved expenses remain payable.

What happens if the work goes beyond the original scope?

I will flag the change. We can decide whether it replaces another task, is added to the scope, is quoted separately, or is scheduled for later.

How do revisions and final deliverables work?

Reasonable revisions can be included when they are part of the agreed deliverable. Changes that expand the original direction may become additional scope.

Privacy, portfolio & delivery

Who owns finished work?

Client-specific final deliverables that have been fully paid for are generally intended for the client's use unless a written agreement says otherwise. My pre-existing templates, methods, reusable systems, and independently developed materials remain mine unless we specifically agree otherwise.

Can completed client work appear in your portfolio?

I may ask to feature selected completed work, excerpts, screenshots, or adapted examples. Confidential, sensitive, private, or identifiable client information will not be intentionally published without appropriate permission.

How do you handle confidential information, account access, and client data?

I use client information only for legitimate work connected to the engagement, keep access limited to what is needed, and avoid unnecessary sharing or exposure of sensitive data. More detail is available in the Privacy Policy.

Do you use AI, and can a client limit or decline its use?

I may use AI-assisted tools for appropriate support tasks when that fits the work. A client can ask that AI not be used, or can set limits on where it may be used.

What work still needs client approval?

Anything that represents the client's business publicly or creates a commitment on the client's behalf should be approved when appropriate, including final pricing, public claims, publishing, purchases, or other owner decisions.

Can you work with more than one client at the same time?

Yes. Unless a signed agreement says otherwise, engagements are independent and non-exclusive. I protect confidentiality, keep client information separate, avoid conflicts of interest, and meet agreed commitments.

What happens at handoff or when an engagement ends?

I can provide agreed final files, status notes, trackers, documentation, and a reasonable summary of outstanding items. Access should then be reviewed and removed where appropriate.
