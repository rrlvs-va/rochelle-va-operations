How I Work

Keep the work visible. Keep it moving.

I prefer a simple operating rhythm: capture what needs attention, clarify priority and ownership, execute the work, then close with a clear status update, documented next step, or completed handoff.

Capture

Tasks, requests, deadlines, files, and open questions are logged so important work does not rely on memory.

Prioritize

Urgency, due dates, dependencies, and required approvals are made clear before work starts.

Execute

The task is completed with the needed research, communication, documentation, or coordination.

Close

Status, completion notes, deliverable links, follow-ups, and any remaining decisions are documented clearly before the task is closed or handed off.
